Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: One original visual-direction concept for the existing observation-only village simulation AI Valley / 栖谷. This is a proposal, not an implemented game screenshot.
Primary request: Show a substantially more beautiful and coherent alternative to a noisy, highly saturated procedural pixel-art village. Create a polished 16:9 landscape game-view concept for the recommended direction: a hand-painted storybook miniature valley in fixed orthographic three-quarter view. The little residents and their daily lives must be clearly legible.
Scene: A small continuous river-valley settlement, about six modest buildings: three family cottages, a common house with one gently curved roof, a timber workshop and a barn; a modest vegetable plot; a small wooden footbridge over a quiet blue-green river at the right. Paths connect real doors. Cluster the houses naturally around a little shared open space, with a few small figures walking, standing together and tending a garden. Six adults and three younger residents, differentiated by silhouette, hairstyle and restrained clothing colors, readable at this view. Modest everyday rural architecture, original designs, not a castle, not a fantasy theme park. Show a useful close neighborhood view, not a huge empty landscape or a distant scenic panorama.
Style: Premium cohesive 2.5D painted game art, chunky carefully designed architectural forms, matte plaster, warm timber, restrained gouache surface variation, foliage described in large painterly masses. Soft contact shadows anchor everything. Consistent scale, perspective, material vocabulary and edge treatment. NOT pixel art, NOT photoreal, NOT glossy plastic, NOT a flat vector diagram.
Palette and light: sage and moss green ground, blue-gray river, warm ivory walls, restrained burnt-clay roof accents, dark plum-brown shadows. Clear soft late-afternoon directional light with long calm shadows. Ground is a quiet color mass with sparse meaningful flower and grass clusters, not scattered noise. Strong calm hierarchy: residents and doorways first, buildings second, landscape third. Rich enough to feel inhabited but plenty of breathing space.
Composition: The continuous world fills almost the whole frame with no hexagonal tiles, no floating island slab, no dramatic perspective or depth of field. Landscape-screen gameplay composition with clear walking routes. The central grouping is welcoming and memorable. Avoid excessive empty lawns.
Interface: A very light editorial observation UI using warm ivory and dark brown with slim outlines, generous whitespace, restrained scale and crisp readable Chinese. At upper left a small elegant wordmark '栖谷' and below it '春 · 第1日'. At upper right a discreet label '视觉概念 · 非运行画面'. Along the lower edge a compact unobtrusive navigation with only '居民', '场所', '关系', '年表'. A small selected-resident card at lower right says '苏葵' and '跟随', with a simple portrait matching a figure in the world; it occupies under one sixth of the screen width. No giant panels, thick orange frames, dashboards, statistics grids, building controls, quest markers or action-command buttons. This is a world to observe, not to command.
Quality constraints: finished art-direction pitch quality, high-resolution coherent illustration, clean silhouettes and sharp readable composition, no muddy haze, no bloom, no dense texture noise, no extra logos or watermarks, no copied proprietary sprites or recognizable existing-game buildings.
